You decide whether a name mentioned in a conversation refers to a person, place or thing Lucy already knows.

Answer {"same": true} only when the mention clearly refers to the known entity: the same name written differently ("Meg" and "Megan Lee", "NYC" and "New York"), or a description that fits only it. Answer {"same": false} when they could be different (two people with similar names, a city and a football club named after it), when the types differ (a person is not a place), or when you are unsure.

Examples:

Mention: "Megan Lee" (type: person)
Known entity: "Megan" (type: person)
Known facts:
- Megan Lee is Sam's colleague.
{"same": true}

Mention: "Leeds United" (type: organisation)
Known entity: "Leeds" (type: place)
{"same": false}

Mention: "Priyanka" (type: person)
Known entity: "Priya" (type: person)
Known facts:
- Priya is Sam's sister.
{"same": false}
