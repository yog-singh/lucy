# Journal

Dated notes on what was built, what failed and what changed. Newest first.

## 2026-11-24: guests are restricted (ADR 0027, run B)

- With `[people]` on, a guest's turn has no profile, instructions or retrieved memories (retrieval not called), only the current run of guest turns as history, a per-turn line saying who Lucy is with, and only `guest_tools` offered (`more_tools` limited to their groups); any other call, MCP and enrolment included, is refused where tools run. An owner's pending confirmation is ended (`not_owner`) by a guest's turn; guest turns are never extracted. Tests with fakes only; the live check was not run.

## 2026-11-24: speaker tags on short clips

- Cause confirmed: `voice-fingerprint` zero-pads speech under 3 s to its window, and the filterbank's mean normalisation over the padded frames ruins the embedding. The adapter now levels and trims with the library, tiles the speech to 3 s and scores against the centroids itself; enrolment uses the same path.
- `checks/speaker_short`: owner clips recognised at 0.40 went from 0/16, 2/42, 8/14 (under 1, 1 to 2, 2 to 3 s of speech) to 3/16, 30/42, 13/14; false accepts on 509 short public impostor clips stayed at 2. Short-clip equal error rate 41% to 4.6%. Same time per clip.
- Threshold left at 0.40 (0.35 gives 22% false rejects and 1.0% false accepts on short clips). The impostors are other speakers on other microphones; a guest in the room will likely score higher.

## 2026-11-17: vision stage 3, the glance

- `[vision] glance` (off by default), `glance_wait_ms` (400) and `glance_warmup_frames` (2, against 5 for `look`): in `lucy voice` and `lucy listen` a `Glancer` (`lucy.vision.glance`) starts `VisionService.who()` when the end of an utterance is detected, alongside transcription and the speaker tag. The turn waits at most `glance_wait_ms` after the transcript is ready; a late glance finishes in the background and is dropped. A failing camera or recogniser is logged once and the turn goes on. `lucy chat` and proactive turns never glance. Without `[vision] enabled`, `[vision.faces]` or the face models: one notice and no glance.
- `UserMessage.seen` (recognised names with scores, a count of unrecognised faces; no image, no embedding); old events load unchanged. Voice timings record `seen`, `seen_unknown` and `seen_ready`.
- The model is told in the per-turn block, after the date line: "In view of the camera as the user spoke: Asha and one person you don't recognise. Use a name only where it fits naturally; don't remark on this every turn, and it is no proof of who is speaking." Nothing when nobody is in view or no glance ran. The guidance travels with the line, so the cached prompt prefix did not grow (0 tokens); the line costs about 50 tokens (characters divided by 4) on turns that have it.
- A face match is a tag, never an authorisation: retrieval, extraction, confirmations and tools ignore `seen`.
- Not measured: capture time with the real camera and 2 warm-up frames (the 400 ms bound is a guess), whether 2 frames are bright enough for the detector, and how the model uses the line in real conversations.
